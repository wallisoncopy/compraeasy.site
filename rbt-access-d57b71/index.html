How to get the most out of it

  1. Open the Study Toolkit and pick the 2- or 4-week plan.
  2. Study 4–5 cards a day. After each card, close it and explain the "Tell the difference" box out loud.
  3. Take Practice Exam 1 with a 90-minute timer, then review the areas you missed.
  4. Take Practice Exam 2 a few days later.
  5. The day before the exam, read every "Exam traps" box.
